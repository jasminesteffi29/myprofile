§ 05

Research Projects

Four projects spanning macrophage immunomodulation, mitochondrial telomerase biology, antibiofilm microparticles, and marine-waste biomaterials.

Biosynthesis of Nanoparticles & Immunomodulatory Evaluation

M.Tech thesis · Mammalian macrophage models

  • Investigated immunomodulatory and anti-inflammatory properties of biosynthesized therapeutic formulations using in vitro RAW 264.7 macrophage models.
  • Conducted quantitative cellular assays to evaluate cell viability, cellular protection, and suppression of inflammatory responses.
  • Coordinated specialized analytical characterization (GC-MS metabolite profiling with SITRA) to elucidate the chemical constituents driving functional bioactivity.

Non-Canonical Functions of Telomerase in Mitochondria

Mitochondrial stress & mitophagy in U-2 OS cells

  • Analyzed mitochondrial stress response and mitophagy using mitoKeima assays in the U-2 OS osteosarcoma cell line.
  • Investigated extra-telomeric roles of Telomerase Reverse Transcriptase (TERT) in mitochondrial function.
  • Explored the role of TERT in cancer cell survival and mitochondrial homeostasis.

Alliin–Alliinase Microparticles Against E. coli Biofilm

Presented as "Inhalable Allicin Loaded Microparticle" at ICAMMT'25

  • Developed and evaluated alliin-alliinase microparticle formulations for the controlled generation of allicin as an antimicrobial agent.
  • Investigated antibiofilm activity of the microparticles against E. coli biofilms using in vitro assays.
  • Performed microbiological experiments, biofilm quantification, and data analysis to assess treatment efficacy.

Collagen-Based Scaffold from Marine Waste

Biomaterial processing for in vitro cell culture

  • Fabricated collagen-based scaffolds derived from marine waste for in vitro cell culture.
  • Conducted biomaterial processing and physicochemical characterization.
  • Evaluated scaffold suitability for cell adhesion and growth applications.
